01 / CURRENT WORK2026 — PRESENT

Joining quantum chips, one tiny bump at a time.

Indium micro-bump interconnects for flip-chip and 3D integration of superconducting quantum devices.

IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Quantum chips need reliable connections to other chips. I work on the microscopic metal bumps that bring those chips together.

What goes into it? +

Indium electrodeposition, seed-layer interfaces, lithography, thin-film processing, and microwave-aware integration. I also explore TiN coplanar waveguides and surface-impedance modelling in HFSS.

02 / MSc THESIS2025 — 2026

More sunlight in. Less energy lost.

IZO/Ag/IZO multilayer transparent conducting electrodes for tandem solar cells.

IN PLAIN ENGLISH

A solar cell's transparent electrode needs to carry electricity without blocking sunlight. I investigated ultra-thin layers that improve that balance.

The actual physics +

Optical interference and admittance matching in sputtered IZO/Ag/IZO stacks, evaluated with Setfos and characterization. In the studied 300–800 nm range, the modelled mean transmittance improved from 78.7% to 85.2%.

03 / EARLIER RESEARCH2025

What happens when materials are squeezed?

High-pressure Raman spectroscopy at BARC, and synchrotron X-ray diffraction at INDUS-2.

IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Pressure changes how atoms vibrate and arrange themselves. Light and intense X-rays let us investigate those changes.

More detail +

Pressure-dependent Raman study of Na₂Cu₂TeO₆ using a diamond-anvil cell up to approximately 10 GPa, with related high-pressure XRD experiments at INDUS-2 Beamline 11, RRCAT.
