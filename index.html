About

Hello, I’m Jayesh.

I’m an experimental physicist and Junior Research Fellow at IIT Bombay. My current work focuses on making small indium interconnects that join chips for quantum devices.

My research has also taken me into thin-film optics and high-pressure spectroscopy. I grew up in a village near the hills, and outside the lab I enjoy trekking and photography.
