M.Sc. Thesis · NCPRE, IIT Bombay

Simulation and Optical Design of Multilayer Transparent Conducting Oxides

Investigated IZO/Ag/IZO oxide–metal–oxide electrodes for photovoltaic devices, with emphasis gradually shifting toward photonics and the physical interpretation of light propagation through nanoscale multilayers.

01
Built a SETFOS and Python workflow for transmission, reflection, absorption, photon-flux and theoretical current calculations.
02
Examined non-bulk optical behaviour in sub-10 nm silver using Drude-based interpretation, LSPR considerations and realistic film-growth constraints.
03
Explicitly separated high simulated improvement from realistic post-fabrication performance affected by morphology, roughness and interfaces.
04
Connected optical performance with sheet resistance and Haacke figure-of-merit analysis.
